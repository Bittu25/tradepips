Frequently asked questions

I have never traded. Is that a problem?

No. Class 1 starts with what a currency pair is. If you already trade, classes 6 to 13 are usually where the gaps are.

Do I need money to trade with?

Not during the course. Everything is practised on a demo account with virtual funds. Whether you trade real money later is your call.

Will I make a profit after this?

Nobody can promise that, and be careful of anyone who does. Most retail traders lose money. You will learn a process and how to keep losses small. The results depend on you and the market.

Which markets will I learn?

Forex, gold and crypto. Market structure reads the same way on all three. What changes is how each one moves and how you size the trade, and both are covered.

Which indicators will I learn?

None. You will learn complete market structure on a clean chart: where price has turned, where it broke, and what that says about the next move. That is enough to find an entry, a stop and a target.

Do you give trade calls?

No. This is teaching only. You will not be told what to buy or sell.
